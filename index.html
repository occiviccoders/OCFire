        Before you use California Fire Watch

        We built this to be as accurate and helpful as we can. But it is a volunteer project that only repackages information published by others, and it is only as correct as its sources.

        • Who runs it. California Fire Watch is operated by Initial Apps LLC, a California limited liability company, and built with volunteers from OC Civic Coders. Contact: info@initialapps.com.
        • Unofficial. California Fire Watch is not affiliated with or endorsed by CAL FIRE, the National Weather Service, NASA, NOAA, CHP, Cal OES, any utility, or any other agency.
        • Information, not instructions. Do not use it to decide whether, when, or how to evacuate, and never wait on it to leave. Evacuation orders come from law enforcement and fire officials through official alerts (AlertOC, Genasys Protect, Wireless Emergency Alerts), and those always take priority over anything shown here.
        • Data can be late, incomplete, or wrong. Fire perimeters can lag hours behind the fire. Satellite hotspots can be misplaced, missed, or false alarms. Forecasts are estimates. Suggested routes don't know about live traffic, unreported closures, or what the fire is doing right now. Any source can go down without notice.
